# Duty Day: field work that runs itself

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: final design, not built.

## The idea in one line

The worker taps **one button** in the morning. The system does the rest: it records the route,
checks in and out at customer sites, proves the visit, counts km, makes the travel claim,
approves the clean ones, and builds the month sheet for salary.
Managers only look at **exceptions**, on one live map.

## Rules for every screen

1. **The system acts first.** A person only confirms or fixes.
2. **One tap, one idea.** No forms where the phone already knows the answer.
3. **Colours mean the same thing everywhere:** green = good, blue = moving, amber = check this, red = act now, grey = off duty.
4. **Words a first-time user understands.** Hindi, Marathi, English. Icons beside words.
5. **Works on a cheap phone with bad network.** Offline first. Low battery use.
6. **Never punish on its own.** The system flags. A person decides.

## Big field problems in India this solves

| Problem | What happens today | What the system does |
|---|---|---|
| Proxy attendance (friend checks in) | Trust only | Check-in only inside the fence + optional selfie |
| Fake GPS apps | Not seen | Mock location detected → red alert |
| Inflated km / petrol claims | Worker types km, fights with HR | Km from the real route, capped; claim made by the system |
| "I was at the customer" but was not | No proof | Visit proof: route + time on site + photo + customer WhatsApp tap |
| Personal work in duty hours | Not seen | Unknown stops over 45 min flagged |
| Phone "switched off", "no network" | Excuse accepted | Gap shown with the real reason (battery, network, location off, app killed) |
| Forgot to check in / out at site | Missing data, corrections | Auto site check-in and check-out by fence |
| Forgot to check out for the day | Wrong hours | Auto check-out at last activity + flag |
| Rural area, no internet | Data lost | Saves offline, uploads later |
| Salary disputes (late, half day, travel) | Excel by hand, arguments | Month sheet built from facts; worker sees the same numbers |
| Manager calls everyone "where are you?" | 20 calls a day | Live map shows everyone |
| Customer calls "when will he come?" | Calls the office | WhatsApp "on the way" with ETA |
| Daily report on WhatsApp groups | Chaos | Day summary built by the system |

## What we build

### 1. One-tap duty (field app)

- Big button: **Start duty**. Only works inside a work-place fence (or the first customer site, by policy).
- Optional selfie at start (setting, per role).
- Phone records the route only while on duty: a point every 2 min or 100 m. Offline queue.
- Fixed notice on Android: "OneOhm is recording your duty route".
- End of day: **End duty**. If forgotten, the system ends it at the last activity and flags it.

### 2. Auto site visits

- On duty, the phone enters a customer site fence (radius from policy) → visit **checks in by itself**.
- Leaves the fence → visit **checks out by itself**.
- The worker sees a small card: "At Sharma home, 10:42". One tap to add a photo or note.
- Manual check-in stays as a fallback.

### 3. Customer on the way + visit confirm (WhatsApp)

- Worker starts moving to the next job → customer gets "Ravi is on the way, about 25 min" with a live link.
- At check-out → customer gets "Did Ravi visit you today?" with **Yes** / **No** buttons.
- One customer tap is the strongest proof. We already send WhatsApp.

### 4. Visit proof badge

- **Verified**: near the site for most of the visit, long enough, photo near the site, no fake GPS, or customer said Yes.
- **Doubtful**: any one signal is bad. The reason is shown in one line.
- **No proof**: no location at all.

### 5. Km and travel claim, automatic

- Km from the route, bad points removed. Gaps filled with straight lines and marked.
- **Paid from where duty starts** (home or office), decided 2026-11-16. The straight line runs
  from duty start through each site to duty end.
- Paid km = at least the straight line, at most straight × 1.6 (setting). No site visits = 0 km.
  Under 1 km straight: at most 2 km, always reviewed. Optional commute cut per day (setting, default 0).
- Each person has a base place (HR sets it). A start or end more than 5 km (setting) from the base
  and every office, or no known start or end, sends the day to a manager.
- At End duty, the claim is made by the system.
- **Auto-approve** (off by default) runs each morning for clean days → payable in finance.
- Only flagged days go to the manager, with the reasons shown. Approve or reject in one tap.
- Test without a phone: `pnpm sim:duty` (see `apps/api/test/tools/duty-sim/README.md`).

### 6. Stops and gaps

- Stop = 10 min or more within 100 m. Named: office, customer, or **unknown stop**.
- Unknown stop over 45 min → amber.
- No points for 20 min on duty → gap, with the reason → red if repeated.

### 7. Live map (the "command map", web)

The main screen for managers. Google Maps (already in the web app), clean light style, no clutter.

**On the map**
- Each worker = round photo pin with a colour ring (green on visit, blue moving, amber idle/unknown stop, red alert, grey off duty).
- A short fading tail behind moving workers.
- Small label under the pin: name · km today · battery.
- Customer sites = house pins: planned (outline), in progress (pulsing), done (tick), late (red dot).
- Work places = soft circles (fences).

**Top bar**: counts as chips. On duty 12 · On visit 5 · Moving 4 · Idle 2 · Alert 1. Tap a chip = filter the map.

**Alert feed (right side, like game notifications)**
- "Ravi: unknown stop 50 min" → [Call] [WhatsApp] [Seen]
- "Amit: fake GPS" → [Call] [Open day]
- "Sharma home: no one yet, job at 11:00" → [Send nearest]

**Click a worker** → card: now doing, visits done / planned, km, battery, network, last seen.
Actions: **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Give a job**, **Replay day**.

**Click a customer site** → job card. Action: **Send nearest** — the system lists free workers by ETA. One tap assigns. The worker's phone rings.

**Replay** → a time slider at the bottom. Drag it and the day plays back on the map: route, stops, visits, gaps.

**Updates**: positions refresh every 30 s on the open map. No page reload.

### 8. Month sheet (salary)

- Built every day by the system, not at month end.
- One row per person: working days, present, half days, absent, paid / unpaid leave, holidays, late count, late deduction (rule), hours, travel ₹ (approved), bonuses ₹, doubtful visits, **payable days**.
- If monthly pay is set: gross pay = monthly pay × payable days ÷ days in month.
- The worker sees **the same numbers** in the app ("My month"). No surprise on salary day.
- HR reviews only the amber rows, then **Lock month** → export Excel / send to payouts.
- PF, ESI, TDS and payslips: not in this design.

| A6 | Correction approval: always clears `late`, allows check-out before check-in, allows a correction that writes nothing, allows many per day. (`corrections.service.ts:130,137`) | Recompute `late`. Validate against the merged day. One pending per day. |
| A7 | Check-in on a holiday / weekly off is marked late. (`attendance.service.ts:88`) | No `late` on days off. |
| A8 | Phone uses cached tracking state, so a check-in can go without location. (field `use-workday.ts:29`) | Always read location when consent is on. Server filters. |

**Site visits and travel** (`apps/api/src/modules/site-activity/`, `packages/domain/src/workforce/travel.ts`)

| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | An open site visit can get stuck forever (task handed over, request resolved). Blocks every later check-in. (`site-visits.service.ts:41`) | Visit owner can always check out by visit id. Manager force-close. Auto-close at End duty. |
| S2 | A stop with no location makes both legs next to it pay 0. (`travel.ts:39`) | Skip stops with no location when measuring. |
| S3 | Claim uses **today's** km rate, not the rate on that day. (`travel-read.ts:97`) | Store the rate per duty day. |
| S4 | Unclaimed day becomes worth 0 after the retention period clears coordinates. (`location-retention.ts:50`) | Save the day's km at End duty, before any clearing. |
| S5 | Today can be claimed while the day is still running. (`travel-claims.service.ts:87`) | Claim is made by the system at End duty only. |

| S7 | Task check-in / out is not in a transaction: half-saved visit, retry fails. (`site-visits.service.ts:56`) | Wrap in `db.transaction`, like the service path. |

## Nothing is done by hand that the system knows

Every event moves the next step by itself. No "please tell the office".

| When this happens | The system does this, by itself |
|---|---|
| Worker taps Start duty | Attendance day opens. Route starts. Live map shows the pin. |
| Worker enters a customer site fence | Site visit checks in. **Task starts.** Customer gets "Ravi has arrived". |
| Worker takes photos / ticks the checklist | Saved on the task. Proof badge updates. |
| Worker taps Complete on the task | **Site visit checks out.** Planned visit → done. Customer gets "Work done, did Ravi visit?". Project history updated. |
| Worker leaves the site fence without completing | Visit checks out. Task stays open. Phone asks: "Job not finished. Why?" (3 buttons: come back later / need material / customer not home). Manager sees the reason. |
| Worker starts moving to the next job | Customer gets "on the way" with ETA. |
| Worker taps End duty | Open visit closes. Day km saved. Travel claim made. Clean day → auto-approved → payable → finance told. |
| Worker forgets End duty | Night job ends the day at last activity. Worker and manager are told. |
| Claim approved | Payable made. Finance told. Shown in the bank payout list. |
| Claim paid | Worker told: "₹420 travel paid". Month sheet updated. |
| Leave approved | Month sheet updated. Live map shows "on leave". Work handed over (exists). |
| Any day closes | Month sheet row updates. No month-end Excel work. |

## Nothing goes silent

Each of these makes an alert to a **named person**, with a button to act. If they do not act, it goes up the line next morning (escalation exists today).

| Event | Who is told | Button |
|---|---|---|
| Not checked in by start time + 30 min, no leave | Manager | Call · Mark leave |
| Day not ended (night job closed it) | Worker + manager | Fix times |
| Site visit open over 4 hours | Worker + manager | Close visit |
| Fake GPS | Manager (at once) | Call · Open day |
| Route gap over 20 min | Manager | Call |
| Unknown stop over 45 min | Manager | Call · Seen |
| Visit Doubtful | Manager | Open visit |
| Job left unfinished | Manager | Reassign · Call |
| Planned visit time passed, nobody arrived | Manager + customer told of delay | Send nearest |
| Claim waiting over 2 days | Approver (escalates) | Approve · Reject |
| Claim approved, not paid in 7 days | Finance | Pay |
| Background job failed | Admin | Open job log |

## One place for each person

No jumping. Each role has **one home**. Everything they need opens there, in a side panel, without leaving the page.

**Worker → field app "Today"** (one screen)
- Top: Start / End duty, km today, time on duty.
- Middle: next job card (address, Navigate, Call customer). When on site, the card becomes the job: photos, checklist, Complete.
- Bottom: "My month" strip: payable days, travel ₹ this month.

**Manager → web "Live map"** (new home for field managers)
- The map, the status chips, the alert feed with buttons.
- Click anything (worker, site, alert) → side panel with the day, the job, the proof, and actions. No other page needed.

**Approver → web "Today → Approvals"** (exists, the one inbox)
- Only flagged travel claims and corrections reach here. Clean ones were auto-approved.
- Alerts open **this inbox**, not the Team tabs, so there is one place to decide. Team tabs become read-only history.

**Finance → web "Finance → Payables"** (exists)
- Approved travel arrives with the employee as payee. Pay by the bank flow. Worker is told.

**HR → web "Team → Month sheet"** (new)
- One table. Amber rows first. Click a row → side panel with that person's month: each day, visits, km, leave, flags. Fix there. Lock month.

## Risks

- iPhone needs "Always allow location". Apple checks this strictly.
- Android battery savers (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo) kill the app. The setup screen guides the worker once.
- Battery: 2 min / 100 m is the balance. Setting can change it.
- Google Maps cost grows with live map use and ETA calls. Cache and limit.
- Privacy (DPDP Act): duty hours only, new consent words, fixed notice, retention. Already in place; we extend it.

## Design quality bar

- One design language from `packages/design-system`. No one-off screens.
- Every screen has a loading state, an empty state with a next step, and an error state that says what to do.
- Every number on a screen can be tapped to see where it came from.
- Every automatic action is written to history: who or what did it, and when.
- Test on a cheap Android phone (2 GB RAM) with 2G-speed network before calling a step done.
